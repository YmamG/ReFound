Small acts. Happy returns.

Things get lost.
People bring them back.

ReFound is a community board for belongings that have taken a little detour. Built with campus life in mind, and open without an account.

1. Tell the story

Share a lost or found item with its general appearance, an approximate area and the date. A photo is optional. Leave exact addresses, personal contact details and unique proof of ownership out of the public description.

2. Explore the connections

Search and filter the board, or view potential matches. Suggestions compare category, words, color, general area and date. They are clues, not proof that two reports describe the same item.

3. Talk privately

Send a private message from a report page. The owner reads it using their management key and can reply to the email you provide. There are no automatic email notifications; owners need to check their inbox.

4. Close the loop

Once the item is returned, update the report to Resolved. You can also permanently delete your report, its photo and private messages.

Privacy & safety

Share enough to connect.
Keep the important details private.

What people can see

Your item description, photo, category, color, approximate area, date and status are public. Anything typed into these fields is public too.

What stays private

Report contact information is not shown publicly. Claim messages and reply emails are available only to that report’s owner and authorized moderators. Keep passwords, documents and sensitive identifiers out of messages.

Your management key

It is shown once at submission. Save it privately; anyone with it can manage the report. Keys cannot be recovered automatically. Access sessions expire after one hour.

Looking out for each other

Verify ownership privately, meet safely and avoid sending money to claim an item. Report inappropriate content using the link on each report. Moderators review concerns manually.

Deleting removes the report and its messages from the active site. Any configured backups follow the operator’s retention arrangements. Essential session cookies support secure forms and report management; no advertising or third-party analytics scripts are included.
